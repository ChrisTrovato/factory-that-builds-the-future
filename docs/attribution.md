# Attribution and reuse

The Factory is a Codex-assisted learning and portfolio project. Text, code, simulations, diagrams, and generated concept artwork were developed for the project. Concept artwork illustrates imagined facilities and is not documentary evidence of built hardware. Exact available artwork prompts are retained in the provenance files and media prompt records.

Book research entries and the in-site video catalog identify their sources. Linked talks are provided by their original publishers; the repository does not contain copied full talks.

The published `Factory_Thiel_Future_Builders.mp4` includes a continuous Peter Thiel excerpt from PBS NewsHour's 2016 RNC coverage and Carpenter Brut's “Escape From Midwich Valley,” taken from the user's existing local edit. It is retained as a record of the published launch. Prior production records do not document a public synchronization or reuse grant. No blanket license is granted over third-party footage, music, brands, or linked content. The full purchased track and original broadcast are not redistributed here.

`Factory_Make_The_Math_Move.mp4` is the earlier original silent model animation. The source code and generated artwork should likewise not be described as exclusively hand-authored. No general open-source license has been selected for this repository; inspect applicable rights before reusing material.
