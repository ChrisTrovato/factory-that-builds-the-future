What this project is.

The Factory connects 34 introductory and engineering lessons, 36 practice questions, 16 project blueprints, 12 interactive models and games, six guided expeditions, seven industry talks, and a 287-page illustrated field guide. Explore at your own pace. No account or payment is required.

The content and original concept artwork were developed with AI assistance. Worked examples and software behavior have been checked, but this is an independent learning project, not an accredited course or a substitute for formal study, qualified instruction, or engineering validation. Read the assumptions beside each model and the limits beside each source.

The orbital, ocean, lunar, and Martian scenes are illustrations of ideas. They are not photographs or evidence that the depicted facilities exist. Company ambitions, demonstrated capabilities, and hypothetical systems are distinguished in the book.

Your notes stay with your browser.

Lesson notes, completed lessons, project records, practice history, and reader preferences are stored locally in this browser. The Factory does not send those records to an account or a cloud database. Another visitor cannot see your notebook through this website.

Anyone using the same browser profile on your device may be able to see those records. Clearing site data, changing browsers, or using a different domain can make them unavailable. Keep your own backup of work you want to retain.

There are no advertising trackers or analytics scripts built into the Factory application. The hosting provider still processes normal web requests and may retain service or security logs. External source links open other organizations' websites, which have their own policies.

Industry talks, on your terms.

The Signal section features attributed talks from their original publishers. Posters are drawn locally. No YouTube player or thumbnail is loaded until you choose to load a preview or full talk. Loading a player connects to YouTube/Google and shares normal request information, including your IP address and the site origin. Its own privacy policies then apply. The privacy-enhanced player does not mean no data is processed. You can instead open the original source.

Talks are included for education and discussion. Speakers and organizations do not endorse this site. Ambitions and forecasts in a talk are not proof that a technology exists. Videos can become unavailable or have embedding disabled; every card includes its source link.

Arcade runs, control-room experiments, and expedition reflections are session work. Export a record before leaving if you want to keep it. They are not included in the saved-progress backup.

Take your progress with you.

  1. Open Your workshop and choose Export all learning progress.
  2. Save the JSON file somewhere you control. It may contain your personal notes; treat it as private.
  3. On your other browser or device, open the workshop, choose the backup, review the summary, and select Merge this backup.
  4. Reload and check your restored work. Keep the original backup until you have confirmed it.
Coming from the original Vercel address?

Your old progress is still stored under that address. Export it from the original workshop, then restore it here. The new domain does not automatically copy browser storage.

Learn, question, build, repeat.

Use an error as a place to investigate. Test a prediction in a model. Make one small artifact you can explain. Progress here records what you explored; it does not certify competence or a machine's safety.
